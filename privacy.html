Wildcore Guardians · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Wildcore Guardians is a creature battle game made by Riotic Studio. This page explains, in plain words, what the game keeps on your phone, what the advertising and payment services it uses receive, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There's no account. The game never asks for your email, contacts, photos or location. The name you choose for yourself in the game stays on your phone.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Riotic Studio doesn't collect it, and the game has no analytics of our own.
  • The game shows adverts from Google AdMob. They are never personalised: they aren't chosen from what you do in other apps or on websites, and the game never tracks you. To show and measure them, Google receives some information about your phone and the adverts you see. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland you're asked first how your data may be used.
  • You can buy crystals. Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or your Apple account.
  • We never sell your information.

What stays on your phone

Your game (stages, guardians, gold, crystals and settings), the name and emblem you chose, and a record of recent play are saved in the game's own folder on your phone. They are removed if you delete the game. They are not sent to us. The only way the record of play leaves your phone is if you choose to share it with us yourself, for example when asking for help.

Adverts

The game shows two kinds of advert: ones you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ones between battles. They come from Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google (Google Ireland Limited if you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere).

When an advert is asked for or shown, Google's software inside the game receives:

  • your internet address (IP address), from which a rough location such as your country or city can be worked out;
  • your phone's model, iOS version, language and screen size, and an identifier for this installation of the game;
  • which adverts were shown, for how long, and whether you tapped them;
  • technical reports about the advert software, such as how long an advert took to load or whether it failed.

The game doesn't ask your iPhone for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it doesn't pass your iPhone's advertising identifier to Google.

The game asks Google for non-personalised adverts only, suitable for everyone: they're chosen from things like the game itself and your rough location, never from a profile of you. Google uses the information above to deliver adverts, to limit how often you see the same one, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in its privacy policy.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your choices the first time it runs, before any advert is requested. Storing or reading information on your phone for adverts happens only with your consent. You can change your choices at any time under Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings. If you don't consent, you may still see adverts, with less information used to choose them.

Purchases

Crystals are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so the game can add your crystals. We never receive your name, your card details or your Apple account. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

What Riotic Studio collects

Nothing from the game itself. We have no servers for it and no analytics of our own. If you write to us, we keep your email and what you send us for as long as it takes to help you.

Your choices and rights

  • Adverts: change your choices under Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings (shown where the law gives you this choice). Buying any pack of crystals ends the full-screen adverts between battles.
  • Delete your data: deleting the game deletes everything it saved on your phone.
  • Data Google holds: see and manage it with Google's own tools, such as adssettings.google.com, or ask us and we'll help you reach the right place.
  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to ask for access to your data, to have it corrected, deleted or restricted, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Wildcore Guardians isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the new version of the game is released.

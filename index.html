Wildcore Guardians

A real-time creature battler for iPhone. Send your guardians onto the field, evolve them in the middle of the fight, unleash their Wild Powers and bring down the Blight's fortress, in battles of three minutes or less.

Help and questions

Write to us at support@rioticstudio.com. It helps if you tell us your iPhone model and the version of the game shown at the bottom of its Settings.

Common questions

Does the game save automatically?
Yes, after every battle and every change. Your progress is kept on your phone, so deleting the game also deletes your progress.
I bought crystals and they didn't arrive.
Close the game completely and open it again: a purchase that was paid for is delivered the next time the game starts. If they still haven't arrived, write to us with the date of the purchase.
How do I get fewer adverts?
Adverts you watch for a reward are always your choice. The full-screen adverts between battles stop for good once you buy any pack of crystals.
How do I change my advert privacy choices?
Open Settings in the game and choose Ad privacy choices. See the privacy policy for what the adverts use and why.
How do I change my name or emblem?
Tap your name at the top right of the home screen.
Is it fair?
Yes. How hard a battle is never depends on what you've bought or whether you watch adverts.
